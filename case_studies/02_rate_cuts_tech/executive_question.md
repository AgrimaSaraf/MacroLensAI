# Case 02 — Interest-Rate Cuts and Technology Strategy

## Executive question

**How could falling interest rates affect technology valuations, corporate investment, and strategic priorities?**

---

## Core mechanism

```text
Inflation pressure ↓
    ↓
Expected policy rates ↓
    ↓
Bond yields ↓
    ↓
Discount rates ↓
    ↓
Future cash flows become more valuable today
    ↓
Growth-asset valuations may rise
```

For technology companies, lower rates can also influence:

- Cost of capital
- Venture funding
- M&A appetite
- Enterprise technology budgets
- Hiring
- Cloud and AI infrastructure spending

---

## Strategic implication

The important question is not simply whether technology stocks rise.

The strategic question is:

> **Which business models benefit most from a lower-rate environment, and which decisions should management accelerate or delay?**
