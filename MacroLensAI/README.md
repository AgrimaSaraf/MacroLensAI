# MacroLensAI

**MacroLensAI is a strategy and decision-intelligence project for analyzing how macroeconomic, market, and geopolitical shifts translate into business and investment decisions.**

The project is designed around a simple question:

> **What is changing, why does it matter, what could happen next, and what should decision-makers do?**

Rather than treating AI as the product, MacroLensAI uses structured research, causal reasoning, quantitative analysis, scenario planning, and risk assessment to support strategic decisions.

---

## What this project demonstrates

MacroLensAI is designed to demonstrate four capabilities:

1. **Strategic problem framing**  
   Turn broad macro or market developments into specific executive questions.

2. **Causal reasoning**  
   Connect changes in inflation, interest rates, oil, currencies, growth, and asset prices to business outcomes.

3. **Scenario and sensitivity analysis**  
   Evaluate how different assumptions change the decision.

4. **Executive recommendation**  
   Convert analysis into clear choices, trade-offs, risks, and actions.

---

## Decision framework

Each case follows the same seven-stage structure:

1. **Strategic question**
2. **Hypothesis**
3. **Evidence**
4. **Causal mechanism**
5. **Quantitative test**
6. **Scenario analysis**
7. **Recommendation**

This keeps the analysis focused on decisions rather than on technology for its own sake.

## Example strategic questions

MacroLensAI can be used to explore questions such as:

## Case-study format

Each case study answers five practical questions:

### 1. What happened?
The external development or strategic trigger.

### 2. Why does it matter?
The economic, operational, financial, or competitive mechanism.

### 3. What are the scenarios?
Base, upside, and downside cases with explicit assumptions.

### 4. What are the strategic options?
The realistic choices available to management.

### 5. What should management do?
A recommendation with risks, trade-offs, and monitoring indicators.

## Why MacroLensAI

Most analytical tools stop at describing what happened.

MacroLensAI is designed to go further:

```text
Macro change
    ↓
Business mechanism
    ↓
Financial / operational impact
    ↓
Scenario range
    ↓
Strategic options
    ↓
Recommendation
```

The goal is not to predict the future perfectly.  
The goal is to improve the quality of decisions under uncertainty.

## Example case

### Oil shock and airline strategy

**Strategic question:**  
What should an airline do if crude oil prices rise 30% while consumer demand remains uncertain?

**Mechanism:**  
Oil prices rise → jet fuel costs rise → operating margins compress → fares may need to increase → demand may weaken.

**Options considered:**
- Increase fuel hedging
- Selective fare increases
- Reduce capacity on weak routes
- Protect premium and high-margin routes
- Increase ancillary revenue
- Accelerate operational cost initiatives

**Recommendation:**  
Protect strategically important and high-margin routes, selectively pass through fuel costs, increase hedging discipline, and reduce exposure to structurally weak routes.

See [`case_studies/01_airline_oil_shock`](case_studies/01_airline_oil_shock).
