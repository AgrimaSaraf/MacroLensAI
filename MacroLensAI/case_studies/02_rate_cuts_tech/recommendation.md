# Recommendation

A technology company should use a falling-rate environment selectively rather than treating it as a blanket signal to expand.

Priority actions:

1. Reassess hurdle rates for high-return strategic investments.
2. Revisit M&A targets where financing costs materially improve economics.
3. Prioritize AI, infrastructure, and product investments with credible long-term cash-flow impact.
4. Avoid using lower rates to justify weak unit economics.
5. Monitor whether lower rates reflect improving inflation or deteriorating economic growth.

The critical distinction is whether rates are falling because the economy is normalizing or because demand is weakening sharply.
