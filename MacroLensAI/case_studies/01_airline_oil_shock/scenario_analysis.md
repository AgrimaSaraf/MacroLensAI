# Scenario Analysis

## Base case

### Assumptions
- Oil prices increase 15%
- Demand remains broadly stable
- Competitors raise fares selectively
- Currency remains stable

### Expected impact
- Moderate fuel-cost pressure
- Some margin compression
- Limited demand destruction

### Management response
- Selective fare increases
- Tight cost control
- Maintain strategic routes
- Monitor price elasticity closely

---

## Downside case

### Assumptions
- Oil prices increase 30%
- Domestic currency weakens
- Consumer demand softens
- Competitive pricing remains aggressive

### Expected impact
- Sharp increase in fuel expense
- Lower margins
- Reduced pricing power
- Higher risk on low-margin routes

### Management response
- Reduce capacity on structurally weak routes
- Increase fuel-hedging discipline
- Protect premium and high-margin routes
- Expand ancillary revenue
- Defer non-critical spending

---

## Upside case

### Assumptions
- Oil prices rise temporarily
- Demand remains strong
- Industry pricing remains rational
- Premium travel remains resilient

### Expected impact
- Cost pressure is partially offset by stronger fares
- Margins remain manageable
- High-value routes remain attractive

### Management response
- Preserve capacity
- Focus fare increases on less price-sensitive segments
- Avoid overreacting to a temporary shock
