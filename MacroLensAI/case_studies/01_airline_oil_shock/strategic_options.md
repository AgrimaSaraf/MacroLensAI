# Strategic Options

## Option 1 — Pass fuel costs through via higher fares

### Advantage
Protects unit economics.

### Risk
Can reduce demand, especially among price-sensitive travelers.

---

## Option 2 — Increase fuel hedging

### Advantage
Reduces exposure to further oil-price increases.

### Risk
Can be costly if oil prices fall.

---

## Option 3 — Reduce capacity on weak routes

### Advantage
Protects profitability and reallocates aircraft toward stronger markets.

### Risk
Can weaken network connectivity and long-term market presence.

---

## Option 4 — Protect premium and high-margin routes

### Advantage
Preserves the most attractive revenue pools.

### Risk
Can create overdependence on a smaller group of routes.

---

## Option 5 — Increase ancillary revenue

Examples:

- Baggage
- Seat selection
- Priority boarding
- Loyalty products
- Partner revenue

### Advantage
Raises revenue without relying entirely on base fares.

---

## Option 6 — Accelerate operational efficiency

Potential levers:

- Aircraft utilization
- Crew scheduling
- Turnaround time
- Route productivity
- Procurement
- Maintenance planning
