# Case 01 — Airline Strategy Under an Oil Price Shock

## Executive question

**What should an airline do if crude oil prices rise 30% while consumer demand remains uncertain?**

This case evaluates how an external commodity shock can affect airline economics and what management can do to protect margins without damaging long-term competitive position.

---

## Why this matters

Fuel is one of the largest variable costs for an airline.

A major rise in oil prices can therefore create a chain reaction:

```text
Oil prices ↑
    ↓
Jet fuel costs ↑
    ↓
Operating costs ↑
    ↓
Margins compress
    ↓
Pressure to increase fares
    ↓
Demand may weaken
```

The core strategic problem is not simply whether costs increase.

The real question is:

> **How much of the shock should management absorb, pass through to customers, hedge, or offset through network and operating decisions?**

---

## Key decision areas

Management needs to evaluate:

- Pricing
- Fuel hedging
- Route economics
- Capacity allocation
- Fleet utilization
- Premium versus price-sensitive demand
- Ancillary revenue
- Cost reduction
- Competitive response
