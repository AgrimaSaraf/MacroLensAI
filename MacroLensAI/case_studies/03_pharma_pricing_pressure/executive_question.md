# Case 03 — Pharmaceutical Pricing Pressure

## Executive question

**How should a pharmaceutical company respond when pricing pressure rises in a major market?**

---

## Strategic problem

Pricing pressure can come from:

- Government regulation
- Payer negotiations
- Generic competition
- Biosimilars
- Reimbursement changes
- Greater price transparency
- Procurement consolidation

The business impact can include:

```text
Price pressure
    ↓
Revenue per product ↓
    ↓
Margin pressure
    ↓
Portfolio economics change
    ↓
Capital-allocation choices become more important
```

---

## Key strategic choices

Management may need to reconsider:

- Portfolio mix
- Market prioritization
- Launch sequencing
- Contracting strategy
- Evidence generation
- Cost structure
- R&D allocation
- Partnerships and licensing
