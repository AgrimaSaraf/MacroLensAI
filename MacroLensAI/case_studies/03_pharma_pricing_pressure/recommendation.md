# Recommendation

A pharmaceutical company facing sustained pricing pressure should avoid treating the problem as a pure cost-cutting exercise.

Recommended priorities:

1. Protect differentiated products with strong clinical value.
2. Reallocate commercial investment away from low-return products.
3. Strengthen evidence demonstrating economic and patient value.
4. Reassess market and launch prioritization.
5. Use partnerships, licensing, or portfolio restructuring where internal returns are unattractive.
6. Track reimbursement changes and competitor entry as leading indicators.

The objective is to improve portfolio quality and capital allocation rather than simply defend every product equally.
